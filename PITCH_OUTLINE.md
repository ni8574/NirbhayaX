### 🎴 Slide 2: The Problem — The Broken Pipeline
- **Headline:** Panic Buttons Send Alerts. But What Happens Next?
- **Key Pain Points:**
  1. **Evidentiary Evaporation:** Digital photos and audio recorded during attacks are routinely challenged, claimed as doctored, or deleted under duress.
  2. **The Legal Chasm:** India's new criminal codes (**Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023**) mandate strict **Section 63 Electronic Evidence Certificates**. Victims cannot generate these without expensive forensic lawyers.
  3. **Healthcare Disconnect:** Physical trauma and Medico-Legal Cases (MLCs) remain trapped on hospital paper, isolated from police investigations.
- **Statistic Callout:** *Over 70% of digital evidence in gender-based crime trials suffers from chain-of-custody objections or procedural dismissal.*

### 🎴 Slide 3: The Solution — The Proof-to-Justice Case Journey
- **Headline:** One Incident = One Immutable Case Lifecycle
- **The 5-Stage Architecture:**
  - **Stage 1 (Protection):** One-Tap SOS, WebSocket live telemetry, 15-min check-in countdown timer, Quick Exit disguise.
  - **Stage 2 (Evidence AI):** Pure OpenCV person/face counting, Whisper audio transcription, video keyframing.
  - **Stage 3 (Integrity):** Double SHA-256 hashing, AES-256-GCM off-chain encryption, on-chain smart contract notarization.
  - **Stage 4 (Justice):** Bilingual legal complaint generator (English & Hindi) aligned with BNS 2023 + Section 63 BSA certificate with verification QR.
  - **Stage 5 (Support):** Haversine trauma facility locator, 1-tap ambulance shortcuts, 24-hr cryptographic doctor passes.
